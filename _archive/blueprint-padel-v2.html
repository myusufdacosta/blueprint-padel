The method

Built like a plan,
not a scramble.

Every session works one layer deliberately, so it compounds instead of plateauing.

01

Footwork

Split step, recovery and court movement — the base every shot is built on.

02

Positioning

Where to stand, when to press, and how to read the ball off the glass.

03

Repetition

Deliberate, corrected reps — technique drilled until it holds under pace.

04

Match craft

Shot selection and pattern play — turning clean technique into won points.

Before you book

Good to know.

Do I need a partner or padel experience?

No. Private lessons and the free assessment work solo. Academy groups are matched to your level, so first-timers train with first-timers.

What should I bring?

Yourself and comfortable sports shoes with non-marking soles. Rackets and balls are provided if you don't have your own.

What happens at the free assessment?

Coach Hasan watches you play, works out where your game sits, and recommends the program that fits. No cost and no obligation afterwards.

How do I pay?

Card, cash or EFT. Confirm your session on WhatsApp and Coach Hasan will tell you the amount and how to settle it.

Which venue will I train at?

Blueprint Padel is moving to its Woodstock and Milnerton courts. Message Coach Hasan and he'll confirm which one your session runs at.
